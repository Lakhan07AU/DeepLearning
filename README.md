## 📖 What is Deep Learning?
 
Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning built on **artificial neural networks** with multiple layers ("deep" architectures) that learn hierarchical representations of data — edges before shapes, shapes before objects, words before meaning. Instead of hand-crafting features, the network learns them directly from raw data through repeated exposure and gradient-based optimization.
 
At its core, every deep learning system is doing one thing: adjusting millions of numbers (weights) to minimize how wrong its predictions are — one backward pass at a time.

### Foundations
- Perceptrons & the biological inspiration behind neural nets
- Activation functions (ReLU, Sigmoid, Tanh, Softmax)
- Forward propagation & backpropagation
- Loss functions & gradient descent (SGD, Adam, RMSProp)
- Weight initialization & vanishing/exploding gradients
### Core Architectures
- **CNNs (Convolutional Neural Networks)** — image & spatial data
- **RNNs / LSTMs / GRUs** — sequential & time-series data
- **Transformers & Attention** — the backbone of modern NLP and beyond
- **Autoencoders** — compression, denoising, anomaly detection
- **GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)** — generative modeling
### Training & Optimization
- Regularization (Dropout, L1/L2, Batch Normalization)
- Hyperparameter tuning
- Transfer learning & fine-tuning pretrained models
- Overfitting/underfitting and how to fight both
### Applications
- Computer Vision (classification, detection, segmentation)
- Natural Language Processing (embeddings, sentiment, generation)
- Reinforcement Learning basics
- Multimodal & generative AI
### Ethics & Real-World Context
- Bias, fairness, and accountability in AI systems
- Explainability & interpretability
- Career pathways and industry applications of AI/DL
